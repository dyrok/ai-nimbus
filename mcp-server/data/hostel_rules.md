## 1. Gate Curfew & Late-Entry Passes

- **1.1 Weekday Gate Curfew:** Main hostel residence gates close at **22:30 IST** Sunday through Thursday. Residents must scan their campus ID at the security turnstile upon entry.
- **1.2 Weekend Gate Curfew:** On Friday and Saturday nights (and eve of gazetted campus holidays), the main gate remains open until **23:30 IST**.
- **1.3 Late-Entry Pass Procedure:** Residents returning after curfew for academic lab work, hackathon milestones, or verified travel must log an advance late-entry pass at the Hostel Security Desk before **20:00 IST**. Unregistered late returns are logged for warden review; no automatic grace-period exemptions exist outside a filed pass.

## 2. Visitor Registration & Logbook Hours

- **2.1 Weekday & Weekend Visitor Hours:** Non-resident visitors (including parents, day-scholar project teammates, and visiting students from other hostels) must sign the Main Gate Visitor Logbook with valid photo ID. Weekday visiting hours run **09:00–20:30 IST**; weekend visiting hours run **09:00–21:00 IST**.
- **2.2 Common-Area Restriction:** Visitors are strictly limited to the ground-floor reception lounge, study atrium, and dining hall courtyard. Entry into upper residential wings or sleeping rooms is prohibited without written Warden approval.
- **2.3 Overnight Guests:** Unauthorized overnight stays in student rooms are prohibited. Visiting parents requiring short-term accommodation must book the Campus Guest House via the Student Affairs Office.

## 3. Electrical Appliances & Pantry Safety

- **3.1 Permitted Low-Wattage Devices:** Laptops, phone chargers, desk lamps (LED <= 20W), and certified study monitors are permitted inside student sleeping rooms.
- **3.2 Electric Kettles & Floor Pantry Bays:** Electric kettles rated up to **800W** with automatic shutoff are permitted **only inside designated floor pantry bays**, never inside sleeping rooms or plugged into multi-strip extension cords.
- **3.3 Prohibited High-Load Appliances:** Immersion water heaters, open-coil hot plates, induction cooktops, space heaters, and steam irons are prohibited inside residential rooms due to circuit breaker load limits. Prohibited items discovered during inspection are impounded until semester end.

## 4. Dining Hall & Mess Timings

All residential dining halls operate on **Asia/Kolkata (IST)** schedules:

- **4.1 Breakfast:** `07:30 – 09:30 IST` (Monday–Friday); `08:00 – 10:00 IST` (Saturday–Sunday).
- **4.2 Lunch:** `12:15 – 14:15 IST` (Monday–Sunday).
- **4.3 Evening Tea & Snacks:** `16:45 – 17:45 IST` (Monday–Sunday).
- **4.4 Dinner:** `19:30 – 21:30 IST` (Monday–Sunday).
- **4.5 Mess Rebate Notice:** Academic travel or medical absences exceeding 4 consecutive days require advance mess rebate filing at least 24 hours prior to departure.

## 5. Quiet Hours & Academic Study Norms

- **5.1 Mandatory Quiet Hours:** Quiet hours run **22:00 – 06:00 IST** daily in all residential wings. Speakers, amplified instruments, and corridor gatherings are prohibited during quiet hours.
- **5.2 Shared Reading Rooms:** Floor study rooms remain open 24/7 for silent individual or low-voice collaborative work provided corridor doors remain closed after **22:00 IST**.
- **5.3 Lab & Emergency Maintenance:** Urgent electrical or plumbing issues inside rooms must be reported to the Ground Floor Caretaker Desk rather than attempting DIY wiring or fixture repairs.
